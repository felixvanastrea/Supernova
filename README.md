# Supernova

A pixel-art space game for your phone. Start as a speck of stardust, merge with planets your size, dodge the bigger ones, and grow into a star. Merge two stars to go supernova.

Play it in the browser: https://felixvanastrea.github.io/Supernova/

## How to play

- Drag anywhere to steer
- White ring: your size, touch it to merge and grow
- Red ring: bigger than you, one hit cracks your shield, the next ends the run
- Meteors kill instantly, watch for the red warning line
- Every 5 supernovas you pick a power and travel to a new world

The whole game is a single file, `index.html`. No build step.

Music: an original chiptune arrangement of *Korobeiniki*, a Russian folk song from 1861 (public domain).
